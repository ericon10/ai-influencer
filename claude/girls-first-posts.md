# first posts: callie, dani, solei

27 posts drafted from `line-bank.csv` (fits = girls/both, no review_flag). every line is secondhand: a friend, roommate or dm, never the girl herself (all three have jobs). each post opens on that person's worst moment. no bank id is used twice.

**format key:** *finished damage* is the @jesqvv shot: selfie cam in a car, wind, blank stare, sad song, ~7s, no talking. on-screen text is one finished event with its petty reason, plus an emoji that doesn't match. song, face and emoji disagree on purpose. *slideshow* has one bank entry per slide and every slide is its own peak. *meme setup* is a two-line setup and punchline.

## lenses

- **Callie**: infuriated. bosses & layoffs. the petty, cruel things employers did to her friends: fired over minutes, laid off at the worst possible moment, time off denied for no reason.
- **Dani**: sad / crying. family & money. the people she loves breaking: new moms losing houses, parents calling them useless, paychecks that don't cover gas.
- **Solei**: frustrated. entry-level, interviews, ghosting & AI. the hiring maze: 3 interviews for costco, rescinded offers, "entry level" that wants 5 years, AI doing the rejecting.
