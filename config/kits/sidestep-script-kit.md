# SideStep Script Kit v1

For AI creator accounts posting job-search content. Use with any caption, storytime, slideshow or talking video. Version 1, Oct 13 2026.

How to use it:
1. Pick a hook from the vault.
2. Rewrite it in the character's voice (voice cards below).
3. Pull real wording from the buyer-language bank.
4. If the post mentions SideStep, stay inside the claims sheet.
5. Keep the 80/20 mix: about 80% pure value and relatable posts, 20% soft product mentions.

---

## 1. Claims sheet (what we can say about SideStep)

Confirm each line before we scale. Nothing outside this list goes into a script.

What it is:
- A Chrome extension for job seekers.

What it does:
- Scores how well your resume matches a specific job posting.
- Tailors your resume to each job using only your real experience. It never invents skills or jobs.
- Fills in job applications in one click.

Price:
- $99 once for a full year. No subscription.

How to say it (approved phrasings):
- "a Chrome extension that tailors your resume to each job using only your real experience"
- "it shows you your match score before you apply"
- "one click and the application is filled"
- "$99 for the year, no subscription"

Never say:
- Any promise of interviews, offers, callbacks or a "% more interviews" number.
- That it "beats" or "tricks" applicant tracking systems.
- That it writes experience you don't have.
- Fake results from a character ("I got hired in 2 weeks with SideStep").

Safe framing: tips and observations ("now she checks her match score before she applies"), not results claims.

Where the link goes: in the post caption or bio, never on the video. Each account gets its own tracked link or promo code.

## 3. Hook vault (50)

Grouped by type. Every hook opens on conflict, a surprise or a question. Never "hey guys" or "today I want to talk about."

2. They ghosted her after the final round, then reposted the job as "urgently hiring."
3. Entry level. 5 years experience. Pick one.
4. Recruiter called my roommate "a perfect fit" and then never called again.
5. My coworker did a 6-hour unpaid "take-home project" for a job that didn't exist.
6. Got rejected at 2:14am. Who is up rejecting people at 2:14am?
7. Five rounds of interviews for a role that pays less than my old one.
8. She got "overqualified" and "underqualified" in the same week.
9. The salary range said "competitive." Competitive with what?
10. My friend's resume went into a black hole and came back as a LinkedIn ad.

### Insider secret
11. Most resumes are rejected before a human ever reads them. Here's why.
12. A recruiter told me the real reason they never called back.
13. That job posting you applied to might not even be real.
14. The first 48 hours after a job posts matter more than your resume.
15. Hiring managers spend about 7 seconds on your resume. Here's what they look at.
16. Why "Easy Apply" is the hardest way to get a job.
17. The words in the job posting are the cheat code. Nobody tells you this.
18. If a job has been "reposted" for 60 days, read this before you apply.
19. What recruiters mean when they say "we'll keep your resume on file."
20. Your resume isn't bad. It just doesn't match.

### Myth bust
21. Stop sending the same resume to every job.
22. A longer resume is not a better resume.
23. "Just network" is useless advice if you don't know anyone. Try this instead.
24. Cover letters aren't dead, but generic ones are.
25. Applying to more jobs is not the answer.
26. You're not unemployable. Your resume is just invisible.

### POV

### Contrarian or curiosity

35. The job market isn't broken. The way we apply is.

### Quick tips (value posts)

### Soft product (the 20%)

## 4. Voice cards

Each character rewrites the same hook in her or his own voice so 30 accounts never sound like one.

### Callie (live)
- Who: 24, recently graduated, figuring out the job market, sunny and a little chaotic.
- POV: "my friend / my roommate / me" storytime. Relatable, never preachy.
- Talks like: casual, short sentences, a bit dramatic for laughs. "be so for real," "I'm crying," "not me doing..."
- Humor: self-roasting, laughing at how absurd the job market is.
- Emoji: 😭 💀 🥲, one per caption, at the end.
- Never: corporate words ("leverage," "synergy"), hard selling, lecturing.
- Pillars: storytime, relatable pain, small wins, soft product.
- Example (hook 3): "entry level but they want 5 years experience. I'm 24. Be so for real 😭"

### Proposed: "The ex-recruiter" (to build)
- Who: early 30s guy, used to work in recruiting, now spills what really happens.
- POV: insider. "Here's what I saw behind the scenes."
- Talks like: calm, dry, confident, a little blunt. Short punchy lines.
- Humor: deadpan.
- Emoji: rarely, 👀 at most.
- Never: hype, "hustle" talk.
- Pillars: insider secrets, myth busts, ghost jobs, what recruiters really mean.
- Example (hook 19): "'We'll keep your resume on file.' I said that 400 times. There was no file 👀"

### Proposed: "The career switcher" (to build)
- Who: late 20s woman leaving a job she hates for a new field.
- POV: "I'm doing this in real time, come with me."
- Talks like: honest, encouraging, practical. Shares what she's trying.
- Humor: warm, a little sarcastic about corporate life.
- Emoji: ✨ 🙃
- Never: toxic positivity, fake success stories.
- Pillars: quick tips, process diaries, "what I'd do differently," soft product.
- Example (hook 45): "Your resume should change for every job. Here's how I do it in 5 minutes before work ✨"

## 5. Weekly rhythm (per batch of accounts)
- Day 1: pick 10 to 15 hooks, write variants per character voice.
- Day 2: make or reuse clips (reaction library, desk shots, lifestyle stills).
- Days 3 to 4: caption, edit, QA, schedule.
- Weekly: rank accounts by signups per tracked link, not views. Clone what sells, pause what only gets views.
